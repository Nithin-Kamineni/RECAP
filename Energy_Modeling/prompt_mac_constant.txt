TASK: audit the two library constants that set the DENOMINATOR of every ECC

at the end of 03_staged_implementation_plan.txt.

WHY. An ECC saving is reported as saved_uJ / total_uJ. The saved uJ come from
weight traffic (DRAM parity, on-chip weight reads) and do not depend on the MAC
cost at all; the total does. On eyeriss_v2_like / resnet18 the MAC is 44% of
the run at 1.16877 pJ per 8-bit MAC (timeloop-mapper.ERT_summary.yaml,
system_top_level.mac, action `compute`). That number comes from Accelergy's
Aladdin plug-in for `class: intmac`: its table has one integer multiplier entry
(32-bit, 40 nm) which Accelergy scales to 8-bit / 45 nm. Horowitz, ISSCC 2014
("Computing's energy problem"), gives an 8-bit integer multiply at 45 nm as
roughly 0.2 pJ and an 8-bit add as 0.03 pJ; the Eyeriss v1 paper's own
normalised table puts a MAC at about 1x an RF read, while this model has
MAC / RF-read = 27.6x. If the MAC is 4-6x too expensive, every percentage in
Tasks 1, 2 and 3 is diluted 1.5-2x, for embedded as much as for recon.

The second constant is DRAM: `class: DRAM, type: LPDDR4` costs 512 pJ per
64-bit word = 8 pJ/bit, flat, from CactiDRAM. It cuts the other way: a cheaper
DRAM SHRINKS the embedded-vs-conventional saving (which is DRAM traffic) and
raises the on-chip share that recon works on.
