EYERISS V2 MODEL CHECK
----------------------
✅ It is a DENSE Eyeriss v2: own hierarchy (16 clusters x 12 PEs x SIMD-2 = 384 MACs,
   72 kB iact GLB + 120 kB psum GLB, no weight GLB, 96x24b weight spad = 288 weights/PE,
   24 B iact spad, 32x20b psum spad, 20 b psums, RS+ spatial constraints). It is NOT the
   stock eyeriss_like renamed.
⚠️ Not modelled, by design or by tool limit: CSC sparsity (dense on purpose), HM-NoC
   router energy, NoC mode switching, 12 b register words (declared 24x8b), 65 nm (45 nm
   used), 200 MHz (1 GHz cycle used for leakage)
⚠️ Order-of-magnitude sanity vs the paper only (3.3 pJ/MAC here at 45 nm without NoC,
   vs ~11 pJ/MAC published at 65 nm with everything); no direct validation possible
