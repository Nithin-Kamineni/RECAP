an identical name, so NOTHING IS EVER REPLACED and no reader can hold a handle
to a file about to be unlinked. A second writer losing the race is the normal
case, not an error. `_write_atomic` stays for genuinely single-writer paths.

2026-09-13  GROUP RESIDENCY: REPORTED, NOT REFUSED (the user's decision)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
After Phase C2 the mobilenet_v2 figure carried R1 and nothing else: R2-R5a came
back `unsupported`, with the reason "infeasible local placement: N (layer,
stage) pair(s) keep fewer than G_rec = 9 weights resident".

WHAT G_rec IS. The embedded layout is ONE BIT STREAM cut into 63-bit codewords.
63/8 = 7.875 is not a whole number, so codewords drift across weight boundaries
and the worst-aligned one reaches into ceil(63/8)+1 = 9 weights. G_rec is that
9: the weights whose retained bits a single codeword touches.

WHAT CHANGED, AND IT WAS REAL. Under the declared off-chip limit the
EDP-optimal plan for ONE layer shrank its weight tile:

    features.1.conv.0.0   filter_glb resident weights:  12 -> 6     (G_rec 9)

Every other depthwise shape stayed at or above 9; most went UP. resnet18 has no
layer below G_rec at all, which is why it kept all five bars. R2/R3 failed on 1
layer of 31, R4 on 4, R5a on 14 -- and R5a had ALREADY been `unsupported`
before Phase C, so only R2/R3/R4 were new.

THE DECISION (the user's, and it is about their hardware, not about Timeloop).
The refusal assumed an engine that can only rebuild from weights co-resident in
the level AT ONE INSTANT. RECAP's reconstruction engine does not work that way:
it accumulates the retained bits as they arrive and rebuilds when the group
completes, so a level holding 6 -- or 1 -- still feeds it, over more accesses
and with more buffering. A small resident tile is therefore a COST, not an
impossibility, and deleting the bar overstated what the hardware cannot do.

WHAT LANDED. `ECC_RECON_REQUIRE_GROUP_RESIDENCY` (env.sh section 4, default 0).
At 0 the shortfall is MEASURED and REPORTED and the bar is charged; at 1 the old
refusal comes back for an engine with no group buffer. The number is not lost --
every bar's record carries `layers_below_G_rec`, `infeasible_layers` with the
per-layer resident counts, and a `group_residency_note` naming the layers and
the fewest weights any of them holds, because THAT NUMBER BOUNDS THE BUFFER the
engine needs. Same rule PE-count differences already follow: report, do not
refuse. test_a_pe_local_boundary_is_rejected_when_the_tile_is_smaller_than_g_rec
now asserts BOTH readings, and its breakage is a shortfall that is neither
refused nor recorded -- the one outcome neither reading allows.
